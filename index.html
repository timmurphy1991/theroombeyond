05 / FAQ

good questions.

Will I need planning permission?

Usually not. We design every studio to sit within permitted development rules. Your property and plot can change that, so we check yours with you before anything is ordered.

Do you build near me?

Yes. We install across the whole of the UK.

Is it comfortable in winter?

Yes. Full insulation, high-performance glazing and heating keep it warm in January and cool in July, so it works as a real room all year.

What will it cost?

Choose your size and finishes in the configurator and the price updates as you go. No call needed.

How long until it's finished?

It depends on your site, groundwork and spec. We confirm a clear timeline before we start, so you know exactly what to expect.

What's the first step?

Design your studio in the configurator and send it to us. We'll talk through your garden and your plans, then map out what happens next.
