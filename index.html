05 / FAQ

good questions.

Do I need planning permission?

Our garden rooms are designed to sit within permitted development, so most gardens won't need it. We check your site before you commit, and tell you plainly if yours is the exception.

How much does a garden room cost?

It depends on size and cladding. Once we know your garden and your choices, you receive one clear, fixed quote with nothing hidden.

Can I use a garden room all year round?

Yes. Layered insulation in the walls, roof and floor, high-performance glazing and built-in wifi heating keep it comfortable in every season.

What does a garden room include?

Everything you need to move in: a birch ply interior, flooring, lighting, sockets, climate control and installation by our own team. Nothing is left for you to finish.

Can I choose the size and finish?

Our garden studios come in three sizes, each clad in natural or charred Thermowood. If you need something different, talk to us and we'll see what's possible.

How is a garden room built and installed?

We design it with you, build it in our workshop and install it ourselves. Because most of the work happens off site, your garden stays calm and tidy.
