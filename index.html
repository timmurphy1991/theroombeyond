05 / FAQ

good questions.

Will I need planning permission?

Often, but it depends on your property, its location and the position and size of the studio. We design around permitted development limits and check your circumstances with you before anything is ordered. Some sites will need planning permission.

Do you build near me?

Yes. We deliver and install across the UK, with the site and access checked as part of the planning process.

Is it comfortable in winter?

Yes. Insulated walls, roof and floor work alongside high-performance glazing and integrated heating to make the studio comfortable throughout the year.

What will it cost?

Studios start at £15,000, with the final price depending on size and finish. Choose your options in the configurator and see the price update as you go.

How long until it's finished?

Timing depends on your site, groundwork and specification. We confirm the programme with you before work begins.

What's the first step?

Start with the configurator or tell us about your garden. We'll talk through the options, check what is possible and explain the next steps.
